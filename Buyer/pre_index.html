Frequently Asked Questions

How does the livestock trading process work?
Our platform connects buyers and sellers directly. Sellers list their livestock with detailed information and pricing, while buyers can browse, compare, and make purchases. We facilitate the entire transaction process, including payment processing and logistics coordination.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept various payment methods including bank transfers, credit cards, and secure digital payments. All transactions are protected by our secure payment system to ensure safe and reliable transfers.
How do you ensure livestock quality?
We have a strict quality assurance process. All sellers must provide detailed health records and certifications. Our team of experts conducts regular inspections and verifications to ensure all livestock meets our quality standards.
What happens if I'm not satisfied with my purchase?
We have a comprehensive buyer protection policy. If the livestock doesn't match the description or there are quality issues, you can initiate our resolution process within 48 hours of delivery. Our team will investigate and ensure a fair resolution.
How do you handle shipping and delivery?
We work with specialized livestock transportation partners who ensure safe and comfortable delivery. All transportation follows strict animal welfare guidelines and includes proper documentation and insurance.
